Modul Sebut HargaQuotation Module

Hantar sebut harga kepada pelanggan, kemudian tukar terus kepada tempahan atau resit. Send price quotes to customers, then turn them straight into a booking or receipt.
Cara Guna / How it works

1Cipta / Create

Menu Sebut Harga → tambah. Isi pelanggan, perkara (aktiviti / penginapan, tarikh, harga) dan Terma & Syarat.

Quotation menu → add. Fill in the customer, items (activity / accommodation, dates, prices) and Terms & Conditions.

2Hantar PDF / Share the PDF

Muat turun PDF Sebut Harga dan hantar kepada pelanggan (WhatsApp / e-mel).

Download the quotation PDF and send it to the customer (WhatsApp / email).

3Ubah jika perlu / Edit if needed

Pelanggan minta ubah? Edit Sebut Harga, atau lanjutkan tarikh "Sah Sehingga" jika sudah tamat.

Customer wants changes? Edit the quotation, or extend the "Valid Until" date if it has expired.

4Pelanggan setuju → Tukar / Agreed → Convert

Tekan Tukar kepada / Change to. Semua butiran diisi automatik.

Tap "Change to". All the details are filled in for you.
