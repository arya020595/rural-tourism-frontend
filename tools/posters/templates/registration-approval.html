UNTUK PERSATUAN · FOR ASSOCIATIONS

Kelulusan PendaftaranRegistration Approval

Persatuan kini meluluskan pengendali baharu sebelum mereka boleh guna RuTEC. Associations now approve new operators before they can use RuTEC.
Cara Ia Berfungsi / How it works

1Pengendali mendaftar / Operator registers

Pengendali memilih persatuan semasa mendaftar. Akaun berstatus Menunggu dan belum boleh log masuk.

The operator picks their association when registering. The account is Pending and can't log in yet.

2Notifikasi loceng / Bell notification

Persatuan menerima notifikasi "Pendaftaran baharu". Tekan untuk terus ke halaman Kelulusan Pendaftaran.

The association gets a "New registration" notification. Tap it to go straight to Registration Approvals.

3Semak & putuskan / Review & decide

Tekan ✎ untuk lihat butiran syarikat, pemilik dan 4 dokumen, kemudian:

Tap ✎ to see the company, owner and 4 documents, then:

Luluskan / Approve
Tolak / Reject · sebab wajib · reason required

4Pengendali dimaklumkan / Operator is informed

E-mel dihantar kepada pengendali. Setelah diluluskan, mereka boleh log masuk dan mula guna RuTEC.

The operator is emailed. Once approved, they can log in and start using RuTEC.
